03Voice

Same language, dois sotaques.

Portuguese isn’t one audience. A caption that sounds right in Lisbon can sound stiff in São Paulo, and the other way round. I grew up with the European one and I know where the Brazilian one differs, so a global brief gets localised twice, not translated once.

Spec copy written for this portfolio. Not client work.

Glass skin in three steps: cleanse, tone, moisturise. And don’t even think about skipping sunscreen. Save this post for your morning routine. Free shipping this week with code GLOW.

  • The global line as it arrives: clear, neutral, written for nobody in particular.
  • Three things to localise: the verb people tap, the words at checkout, and how familiar the brand is allowed to sound.

Pele de vidro em três passos: limpar, tonificar e hidratar. E nada de pular o protetor solar, hein? Salva esse post pra sua rotina da manhã. Frete grátis essa semana com o cupom GLOW.

  • Salva, not guarda: the verb Instagram itself uses in Brazil.
  • Pra, esse, hein? Brazilian feeds are written the way people talk.
  • Frete and cupom are the words on a Brazilian checkout page.

Pele de vidro em três passos: limpar, tonificar e hidratar. E nada de saltar o protetor solar! Guarda esta publicação para a tua rotina da manhã. Portes grátis esta semana com o código GLOW.

  • Tu, not você: brands in Portugal speak to you informally, in the second person.
  • Portes and código: what a Portuguese checkout says.
  • Fewer contractions, and the humour runs drier.

And the calendar flips: a December sunscreen push is a summer story in Brazil and a winter one in Portugal.

04Routine

A content routine, in seis passos.

Good content, like good skin, is mostly routine. This is the order I work in. Pick a product off the shelf.

Step 01 / Cleanse

Research

Clear away the assumptions first. What is this audience already watching, saving and asking about? Which trends are worth joining, and which are already over?

In practice Reading the whole report before deciding which single finding becomes the post.

Step 02 / Tone

Angle and voice

Balance the brief with the audience. One idea per post, in the words that market really uses.

In practice Adapting global English campaigns so they read as if they were written in Portugal.

Step 03 / Essence

Script

The concentrated part. A hook for the first second, then only what earns its place. If a line can go, it goes.

In practice Scripts and captions for reels, expert videos and carousels.

Step 04 / Serum

Direction

Targeted treatment: the plan for the shoot, the angles, the b-roll and the imagery that carry the line.

In practice Proposing shots, angles and b-roll for each video before it reached the edit.

Step 05 / Moisturise

Refine with editors

Seal it in. Review rounds with the video team on footage, subtitles and translation until it reads right.

In practice I haven’t been the one editing so far. It’s the skill I’m adding now.

Step 06 / SPF

Measure and protect

Never skip it. Report what worked, test what might work better, and keep the brand consistent wherever it shows up.

In practice Performance reports for management, and paid social A/B tests to refine creative and copy and lower cost per click.
